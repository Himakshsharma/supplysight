# SOP-VEN-002 Quarterly Vendor Evaluation

*Fictional SOP for the SupplySight demo; synthetic organisation.*

## Purpose
Rate suppliers objectively so that sourcing decisions favour reliable, high-quality vendors.

## Criteria and weights
| Criterion | Weight | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| Quality | 40% | Accepted lots ÷ received lots (SOP-QC-001) |
| Delivery | 30% | Goods receipts on or before the PO delivery date (EKPO-EINDT) |
| Price | 20% | Price versus market index and other vendors |
| Service | 10% | Responsiveness, documentation, COA accuracy |

## Rating bands
- A (≥ 85): preferred vendor, eligible for long-term contracts.
- B (70–84): approved, improvement plan for weak criteria.
- C (< 70): conditional; no new materials, sourcing to start an alternate vendor.

## Lead-time performance
The planned delivery time in the info record (APLFZ) is reviewed every quarter against actual receipt times and updated if the average deviates by more than 20%.
