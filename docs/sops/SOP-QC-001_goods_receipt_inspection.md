# SOP-QC-001 Goods Receipt Inspection for Cosmetic Raw Materials

*Fictional SOP for the SupplySight demo; synthetic organisation.*

## Purpose
Ensure that raw materials and primary packaging meet specifications before use in production.

## At the dock
1. Stores verify the vehicle, seal, PO number and vendor name against the purchase order.
2. Check labels: material name, batch number, manufacturing and expiry dates, net weight.
3. Reject immediately any container that is damaged, leaking or unlabelled.

## Sampling plan
- Raw materials (ROH): sample √n + 1 containers (n = number of containers) for identity testing; full testing per pharmacopoeia or in-house specification.
- Fragrance oils and actives: organoleptic check plus GC fingerprint against the approved standard.
- Primary packaging (bottles, jars, tubes): AQL 1.0 for critical defects, AQL 2.5 for major defects.

## Certificate of Analysis
Every lot must come with a vendor COA. QC compares the COA with in-house results; deviations above specification limits lead to rejection.

## Stock status
Received material is posted to quality-inspection stock. Only QC can release it to unrestricted stock. Rejected material goes to quarantine (SOP-QC-002).
