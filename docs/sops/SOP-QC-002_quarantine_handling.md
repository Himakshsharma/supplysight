# SOP-QC-002 Quarantine Handling and Return to Vendor

*Fictional SOP for the SupplySight demo; synthetic organisation.*

## Purpose
Prevent rejected or suspect material from being used in production.

## Quarantine area
Quarantine is a physically separated, locked area with red "REJECTED" or yellow "UNDER TEST" labels. Access is limited to QC and stores supervisors.

## Placing material in quarantine
1. QC raises a non-conformance report (NCR) with the batch, quantity and reason.
2. Stores move the stock to blocked stock in SAP and physically to the quarantine area within 4 hours.
3. Purchasing is informed the same day so a replacement can be arranged (SOP-PUR-002 if urgent).

## Disposition
- Return to vendor: the buyer arranges the return within 15 days; the vendor issues a credit note.
- Rework or use-as-is: only with written approval of the QC head and R&D.
- Destruction: for expired or contaminated material, with environmental compliance records.

## Vendor impact
Every rejection reduces the vendor's quality score in the quarterly vendor evaluation (SOP-VEN-002).
