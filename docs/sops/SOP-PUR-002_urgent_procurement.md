# SOP-PUR-002 Urgent and Emergency Procurement

*Fictional SOP for the SupplySight demo; synthetic organisation.*

## Purpose
Define when an order may be treated as urgent, how it is approved, and the controls that apply when normal lead times are shortened.

## When procurement is "urgent"
A requirement is urgent when the material will stock out before the vendor's standard planned delivery time (MARC-PLIFZ / info record APLFZ) and production would stop. Typical triggers: unplanned production order, quality rejection of a received lot, or a vendor failing to deliver.

## Approval process for urgent purchase orders
1. The planner documents the reason (production order number, stock-out date) in the requisition text.
2. The buyer contacts the existing approved vendor first; new vendors must not be used for urgent orders unless onboarding (SOP-VEN-001) is complete.
3. Urgent POs up to ₹5,00,000 are approved by the purchase manager; above ₹5,00,000 the plant head must approve in addition, even during weekends.
4. Expedited delivery dates shorter than 25% of the vendor's normal lead time require written confirmation from the vendor and are reviewed by the purchase manager.
5. Premium freight or price premiums above 10% follow the price-change approval in SOP-PUR-006.

## After-hours and weekend orders
Purchase orders may be created outside business hours (before 08:00, after 20:00, or on Sundays/holidays) only for emergencies. The buyer must inform the purchase manager by e-mail the same day. After-hours POs to vendors created in the last 90 days are automatically flagged by the SupplySight anomaly engine and reviewed the next working day.

## Post-facto review
All urgent orders are reviewed weekly by the purchase manager to identify planning failures and recurring causes.
